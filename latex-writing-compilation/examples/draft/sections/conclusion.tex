\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

SAGN keeps long-range electrostatic context in a polymer graph network at a
cost that scales with the sparsified neighbourhood rather than the full pair
set. Under scaffold splits it lowers MAE by 1.33\,K against a budget-matched GIN
baseline. Remaining limitations: the benchmark covers a single chemistry family,
and the low-rank projection assumes a shared basis across monomers, so
multi-family transfer is untested. The full source tree, the scaffold split
indices and the exact build commands accompany this submission.
